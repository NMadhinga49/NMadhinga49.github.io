01 — About

“The future worth building is one where AI and geographic understanding strengthen human judgement, not replace it.”

— Jack Dangermond

I am a Geomatics and Surveying Engineering Honours graduate from Gwanda State University with applied experience across cadastral surveying, engineering setting-out, and geospatial data projects.

My work bridges classical field precision with modern spatial analytics—from boundary subdivisions, Survey Diagrams, and General Plans to robotic Total Station operations, GNSS control networks, and custom coordinate reference systems in QGIS and AutoCAD Civil 3D.

Alongside technical consulting, I founded SpatialTech to deliver accessible online GIS tutoring and practical training programs, helping students and professionals master open-source geospatial tools, Python spatial automation, and development workflows in VS Code.
