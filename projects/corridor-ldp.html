CASE STUDY · 17°48'S 31°05'E · HARARE

Enterprise Corridor Local Development Plan No. 60

Coordinate Reference SystemWGS 84 / UTM Zone 36S (EPSG:32736)
Primary ToolsQGIS, GeoPackage (.gpkg), Print Layout (A3)
Core DeliverablesLand-Use Digitization, Capacity Audit, 30 m Riparian Buffer

1. Objective & Georeferencing

Statutory local development plans often exist as static raster scans that cannot be queried or overlaid with current cadastral and environmental layers. The objective was to georeference Local Development Plan No. 60 into EPSG:32736 with sub-meter control accuracy and construct a clean topological vector database.

2. Spatial Analysis & Riparian Compliance

Land-use zones and road reservations were digitized into a structured GeoPackage. Using vector geometry calculations, residential yield capacity was evaluated across zoning tiers, and a 30-meter riparian buffer was generated along watercourse centerlines to identify encroachment risks.

3. Cartographic Output

The final deliverable was compiled as an annotated A3 cartographic map sheet featuring scale bars, coordinate grids, zoning area breakdowns, and inset context maps for planning review.
