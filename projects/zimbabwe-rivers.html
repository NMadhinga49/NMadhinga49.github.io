NATIONAL HYDROLOGY & DATA VISUALIZATION · ZIMBABWE

Zimbabwean Rivers & Drainage Basin Visualization

High-contrast dark cartography illustrating hierarchical river networks and Zimbabwe's central watershed divide.

Project Overview

Removing political borders, roads, and base-map clutter reveals how Zimbabwe's national boundary and topography are sculpted by water. This minimalist dark-mode visualization renders the country's dense dendritic drainage system using graduated line weights and luminance.

Cartographic Design & Styling

  • Hierarchical Stream Scaling: Applied rule-based and graduated symbology so major perennial arteries—including the Zambezi, Limpopo, Save, Runde, Manyame, Mazowe, and Sanyati—emerge in bright white-cyan while seasonal headwater streams taper into delicate blue filaments.
  • Figure-Ground Contrast: Set against a pure black canvas (`#000000`) to maximize visual contrast and reveal the northeast-to-southwest Highveld watershed divide separating the Zambezi basin from the Save-Limpopo basins.
