Cabo Verde — 2026 FIFA World Cup Thematic Cartography

Multi-scale infographic map pairing an African continental flag-fill choropleth with an archipelago zoom inset and tournament match summary.

Project Overview

Designed as an editorial sports-geography infographic celebrating Cabo Verde's 2026 FIFA World Cup run, this layout solves a classic cartographic scale challenge: displaying a small Atlantic island nation within the context of the entire African continent while keeping every island and capital city legible.

Composition & Cartographic Techniques

  • Continental Flag-Fill Choropleth: Clipped national flag rasters to each African sovereign boundary to create an immediately recognizable continental reference frame.
  • Multi-Scale Archipelago Inset: Linked a callout box off West Africa to a large-scale inset detailing both island chains—the northern Barlavento Islands (Santo Antão, São Vicente, Santa Luzia, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista) and the southern Sotavento Islands (Brava, Fogo, Santiago with Praia, and Maio)—with curved ocean typography.
  • Tabular Attribute Integration: Embedded a structured Group H and Round of 32 match table directly into the print layout to pair spatial context with tournament statistics.
